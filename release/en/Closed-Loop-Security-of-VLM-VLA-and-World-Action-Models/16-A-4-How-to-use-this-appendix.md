## A.4 — How to use this appendix

The closed-loop interface taxonomy, the propagation analysis and the control-selection guide are
unaffected. What changes is the **evidence distribution**: remotely exploitable commercial robot flaws and
embodied attack papers appeared together after the cutoff, suggesting the field's attack focus is moving
from simulation toward deployment. When citing the items above, cite both the retrieval freeze date and
this appendix's date.
