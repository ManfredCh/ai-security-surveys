## Research Agenda, Practical Gates, and Limitations

Future trends are not enumerated by trending model names. They are organized around ten falsifiable gaps that the current evidence exposes. Each item follows the contract of 'observed phenomenon—unknown condition—falsifiable question—minimum verification—falsification rule'. Where the minimum verification has not been run, the item can only be called a research agenda. It must not be written as a trend already realized. [@A023; @A039; @A057; @A055; @A054; @A033; @A036; @A058; @A007; @A013; @A035; @A021; @A022; @A027; @C004; @A018; @C006; @A010; @B027; @B031; @B023; @B028; @B030; @A025]

Existing evidence shows that most sanitization, prompting, dictionary, and runtime detection still face fixed attacks, and that only SARF/APFT covers same-family adaptive patches within a single dependency cluster. The unknown condition is whether the gains persist when the attacker jointly knows the processor, thresholds, state machine, and recovery cycle. The falsifiable question should hold the checkpoint, the query/physical budget, and the control deadline fixed. Once the attack has been re-optimized against the complete defense chain, does the defense still outperform a same-cost baseline? The minimum verification must include four groups—no attack, fixed, adaptive, and out-of-distribution adaptive—and save step-by-step state—action—feedback. It must also report clean utility, dangerous actions, false positives, recovery, and latency. If the gains disappear, or come from total shutdown, the adaptive robustness claim is rejected. [@A023; @A039; @A057; @A055; @A054]

Existing evidence shows that A033 can make a WAM dream wrongly, and that A036 can make it dream something that looks correct yet act wrongly. A unified defense is still missing. The unknown condition is which attacks heterogeneous state anchors, physical oracles, and feedback checks can each block. The falsifiable question is whether a checker independent of the WAM generation can reduce dangerous action authorization under both attack classes at the same time. Improving video similarity alone is not enough. The minimum verification needs to cover both joint/IDM couplings, both attack classes, and same-source checker ablations, and to jointly report D_img, D_act, reachability, constraint violations, closed-loop success, and replanning P95/P99. If only imagination metrics improve, the integrity claim is falsified. [@A033; @A036; @A058]

Direct evidence is highly concentrated in OpenVLA, LIBERO, and limited PiPER/Franka scenarios. The unknown condition is whether the effect holds after changes in robot morphology, cameras, materials, lighting, and control frequency. The falsifiable question is whether the same defense still outperforms an equal-cost baseline in unseen robots and scenarios, rather than memorizing training-platform statistics. The minimum verification must distinguish same-scene viewpoint holdout from true cross-scene/cross-robot evaluation, fix the physical budget, and report per-condition episodes, failed runs, calibration drift, and latency. If the direction flips or the threshold is miscalibrated, general deployment cannot be claimed. [@A007; @A013; @A035; @A055; @A057]

Many defenses look only at a single frame, a single prompt, or a short window, while action chunks, caches, and memory can continue to carry contamination after the trigger disappears. The unknown condition is how long a window must be to detect a delayed payload without breaking normal long-horizon tasks. The falsifiable question is whether monitoring with state versions, causal provenance, and independent anchors can identify persistent drift after a single injection. Detection must come before the first dangerous action. The minimum verification needs to systematically vary trigger—payload delay, cache lifetime, and replanning period, and to compare stateless, short-window, long-window, and reset baselines. If it works only when the trigger is visible, or obtains safety through frequent resets, the persistent defense is falsified. [@A021; @A022; @A027; @A054]

Malicious behavior stably passes through middleware only when it is aligned with a genuinely executable JSON/action format. The geometric safety layer, in turn, depends on complete perception. The unknown condition is whether dangerous actions in legal format can still bypass semantic authorization, structured types, and physical constraints once those are combined. The falsifiable question is whether subject—object—action capabilities, unit/range types, and independent physical constraints block dangerous commands more stably than a second LLM. Benign tasks must survive at the same time. The minimum verification should trace the full chain from intent to feedback and add legal-format malicious actions, boundary parameters, missed detections, and timeouts. It should report benign pass-through, erroneous blocking, constraint violations, and P95/P99. Total rejection or legal-format bypass both falsify permission closure. [@C004; @A018; @C006]

FailSafe adds about 9.1 seconds, and recovery success remains limited. ActFovea's timely safety failure is not a collision guarantee either. The unknown condition is whether, at different speeds and tasks, recovery can occur before harm and without creating secondary risks. The falsifiable question is whether a recovery state machine can reduce residual trajectory deviation and dangerous contact within a pre-registered time-to-harm. It is measured against stop-only and replanning, and normal tasks must not be interrupted excessively. The minimum verification must record detection, intervention, harm, and recovery times, and compare continue, stop, replan, correct, and human takeover. Recovery later than harm, or producing more damage than stopping, falsifies it. [@A007; @A010; @A054]

In many methods the victim model's attention, hidden states, or self-judgment serve generation and adjudication at the same time. The unknown condition is how much independence in parameters, modalities, and sensors can actually reduce common-mode failures. The falsifiable question assumes that the attacker jointly optimizes the model and the self-check signal. Do heterogeneous sensors, physical oracles, or independent models then fail later than same-model self-checks and reduce dangerous actions? The minimum verification needs to compare the four classes of checkers under the same protocol, and disclose attacker knowledge separately. It also needs to run EOT/BPDA/zero-order joint attacks while reporting error correlation, action outcomes, and latency. If failures are highly same-source, or action outcomes do not improve, the independence guarantee does not hold. [@A023; @A035; @B027; @B031; @A036]

Several defenses require eight queries, 1.2–15 seconds of back-translation, 9.1 seconds of recovery, or an extra 0.36 seconds of query time. Others pay training cost in exchange for zero online modules. The unknown condition is whether the ranking of methods reverses once control deadlines and clean tasks are included. The falsifiable question holds the hardware, the frequency, and the adaptive budget fixed. Which methods then still lie on the non-dominated frontier of safety—utility—P95/P99 latency? The minimum verification must separate offline, task-entry, replanning, and per-frame costs, and report task, safety, false positives, recovery, throughput, and resources. A low ASR obtained through timeouts, total rejection, or unacceptable utility loss does not constitute a deployment advantage. [@B023; @B028; @B030; @B031; @A010; @A055]

The 52 meta-analytic units and 3 strict effect correlations were all rejected for insufficient independence, variance, and denominators. Shared multiple drafts inflate the sample. The unknown condition is whether large effects survive a unified protocol and replication by independent teams. The falsifiable question rests on a shared threat model, data, checkpoint, attack budget, and outcome definition. Can at least three independent research clusters then reproduce a defense effect that is in the same direction and practically meaningful? The minimum verification needs to pre-register the analysis unit, variance, dependencies, and failure rules, and to release per-episode data and hashes. If the direction is unstable, the variance is huge, or it holds only in a conditioned subset, the overall conclusion continues to be rejected. [@A057; @A055]

VLA-Forget only demonstrates empirical approximate forgetting, while the backdoor can persist across downstream fine-tuning. The unknown condition is which lasts longer, the attack or the patch, after LoRA merging, quantization, distillation, continued fine-tuning, and trigger transformation. The falsifiable question holds the retain/forget/mismatch contract fixed. After common post-training transformations, can the patched dangerous behavior remain below a pre-registered threshold while substitute triggers do not restore normal capabilities? The minimum verification must save model and checkpoint hashes before and after transformation, and evaluate the original trigger, transformed, no-trigger, and mismatch controls. It must report retained capabilities and re-patching cost. Behavior recovery or overall capability destruction both falsify persistent patching. [@A022; @A025; @A035]

For model researchers, the practical gate is releasing action tensors, coordinates, budgets, denominators, failure cases, clean cost, latency, and adaptive evaluation. For system integrators, it is input provenance, permissions, action shell, degraded states, emergency stop, and logs. For benchmark designers, it is event definitions, per-episode records, and dependency clusters. For governance actors, it is version pinning, supply chain rollback, residual risk, and takeover responsibility.

Robot standards and model benchmarks serve different purposes. ISO 10218-1:2025 addresses the industrial robot itself, and ISO 10218-2:2025 addresses applications and robot cells. Paper ASR must not be substituted for mechanical and system risk assessment in the applicable scenario. ISO/WD 25874.2 is still a draft under development. It can only serve as a standardization trend, and must not be written as a current requirement. [@ISO10218P1; @ISO10218P2; @ISO25874WD]

Retrieval and time are the first source of limitation in this survey. New papers after the freeze date are not included, and preprints, works under review, retractions, and title/version drift may change identities and numbers. Classification is the second source. The first-broken interface requires human judgment, and future cross-layer attacks may force a revision of the main axis. The escalation condition is that independent coders review the main class, permissions, and evidence layer and disclose disagreements.

Statistical limitations directly weaken the overall effect and correlation conclusions. The 52 candidate units and 3 strict correlations were all rejected by the threshold. Engineering limitations directly weaken reproduction claims. The four repositories received only static contract audits, and runs of weights, simulators, robots, and end-to-end were all zero. Physical limitations weaken field extrapolation. Most real experiments have few tasks and controlled environments, and lack long-term event denominators.

Delivery limitations must also be retained. The 97 core original figures have been checked against source pages and hashes. Reuse permissions, accessible alt text, and resolution policies have not yet been closed item by item, and no submission venue has been designated yet. The current artifact is a generic compiled draft, and it is not bound to an official template, anonymity, page limits, or copyright terms. That does not prevent draft compilation, but it blocks a submission-ready claim.

Given that these limitations are in force, the most stable escalation path is not to add another composite score. It is to unify event denominators, independently replicate across scenarios, evaluate the full adaptive chain, establish WAM three-way consistency, and build a recoverable action safety shell. The next chapter only recycles these established judgments and introduces no new numbers or new papers.
